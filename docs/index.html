Record, capture, annotate, and save locally.

wbify screen recorder is a Chrome extension for creating screen recordings, tab recordings, camera videos, screenshots, and annotated visual notes.

RECORDING

Features

Screen and tab recording

Record your screen, browser tab, or camera with optional microphone, webcam overlay, and system or tab audio.

Screenshot capture

Capture the visible page, a selected area, or a full webpage using the built-in screenshot tools.

Markup and editing

Add arrows, boxes, text, highlights, blur, stickers, and crops before saving or downloading your files.

Support

For help, bug reports, or feature requests, contact the publisher from the Chrome Web Store listing account or email the publisher address shown on the listing.

Please include your Chrome version, what you were trying to record or capture, and a short description of what happened.

Privacy

wbify screen recorder is designed for local recording and screenshot workflows. Recordings and screenshots are stored locally in your browser or downloaded to your computer when you choose to save them.

  • The extension does not need an account to record or capture.
  • The extension does not upload recordings or screenshots to a remote server.
  • Screen, tab, camera, and microphone access are used only after you choose a capture or recording action.
